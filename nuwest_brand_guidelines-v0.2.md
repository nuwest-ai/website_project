# nuwest.ai Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity
**Brand Name:** nuwest.ai  
**Logotype:** `nuwest.ai >>`  
**Meaning:** The double chevron `>>` symbolizes forward motion, iteration, and progress — reflecting the brand’s commitment to innovation, speed, and clarity in cybersecurity and AI solutions.

---

## 2. Logo Usage

### **Primary Logo**
- **Text:** `nuwest.ai >>`  
- **Font:** [Chakra Petch](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Chakra+Petch) (Google Font)  
- **Color:** Black (`#111111`) text with bright yellow (`#FFD300`) chevrons.  
- **Spacing:** Maintain at least one chevron’s width of clear space around the logo.  
- **Usage:** Default for light backgrounds.

### **Secondary Logo (Dark Mode)**
- **Text:** `nuwest.ai >>`  
- **Font:** Chakra Petch  
- **Color:** Yellow text (`#FFD300`) with white (`#FFFFFF`) chevrons.  
- **Background:** Charcoal black (`#1A1A1A`).  
- **Usage:** For presentations, videos, or websites using dark themes.

### **Inverted Logo (Highlight Variant)**
- **Text:** `nuwest.ai >>`  
- **Font:** Chakra Petch  
- **Color:** Black (`#111111`) text with white (`#FFFFFF`) chevrons.  
- **Background:** Bright yellow (`#FFD300`).  
- **Usage:** For banners, accent panels, and printed collateral.

### **Logo Files**
- **Logo on bright background:** `logo-bright-background.png`
- **Logo on dark background:** `logo-dark-background.png`
- **Logo on yellow background:** `logo-yellow-background.png`

## 3. Typography

### **Primary Typeface: Chakra Petch**
- **Weights:** Regular, Medium, SemiBold
- **Use:** Headlines, titles, and wordmarks

### **Secondary Typeface: IBM Plex Sans (optional)**
- **Use:** Body text, UI labels, supporting copy

### **Example Hierarchy:**
| Type | Font | Weight | Size | Example |
|------|------|---------|------|----------|
| H1 | Chakra Petch | SemiBold | 48px | nuwest.ai >> redefining resilience |
| H2 | Chakra Petch | Medium | 32px | Cyber resilience beyond buzzwords |
| Body | IBM Plex Sans | Regular | 16px | We build technology that cuts through the noise. |

## 4. Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|--------|
| **Signal Yellow** | `#FFD300` | Accent, chevrons, buttons |
| **Charcoal Black** | `#111111` | Text, primary background |
| **Matte Black** | `#1A1A1A` | Alternate dark background |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Text and highlights on dark backgrounds |
| **Neutral Gray** | `#EAEAEA` | Section dividers, background contrast |

---

## 5. Visual Style
- **Aesthetic:** Futuristic minimalism — sharp edges, geometric spacing, clean contrast.
- **Tone:** Bold, confident, technical.
- **Iconography:** Use outlined geometric icons with consistent stroke weights.
- **Imagery:** Combine abstract AI motifs (grids, data flow, chevrons) with real-world cybersecurity elements (servers, code, terminals).

---

## 6. Applications
- **Presentations:** Use `nuwest.ai >>` logo top-left, with Signal Yellow accents on section headers.
- **Web:** Animated chevrons (`>>`) subtly pulse or move rightward to signify progress.
- **Print Collateral:** Maintain high contrast; no gradients or shadows.
- **Social Media:** Avatar logo may use only `>>` on a black or yellow square background.

---

## 7. Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Direct, informed, confident.
- **Personality:** Analytical yet human — grounded in truth, free of buzzwords.
- **Tagline Example:** *“Cut through the noise. Move forward faster.”*

---

## 8. Do’s and Don’ts
**Do:**  
- Keep spacing consistent.  
- Use approved color combinations.  
- Center chevrons exactly at x-height of text.  

**Don’t:**  
- Stretch or distort the wordmark.  
- Alter chevron proportions.  
- Apply drop shadows or gradients to the logo text.
