# PIR Folder Structure (Recommended)

Use this scaffold both locally and in Claude’s workspace.

```
PIR/
│
├── README.md
├── PIR-project-overview.md
│
├── docs/
│   ├── epistemology-notes.md
│   ├── media-theory-notes.md
│   └── PIR-definition.md
│
├── research/
│   ├── reading-list.md
│   ├── book-excerpts/
│   └── article-excerpts/
│
├── analysis/
│   ├── PIR-threat-model.md
│   ├── aylo-business-analysis.md
│   ├── data-flow-risk-diagram.md
│   └── comparative-frameworks.md
│
├── drafts/
│   ├── essay-v0.1.md
│   ├── essay-v0.2.md
│   └── talk-outline.md
│
└── logs/
    └── YYYY-MM-DD.md
```

This structure keeps philosophical, technical, and analytical components cleanly separated while allowing iterative draft development.
