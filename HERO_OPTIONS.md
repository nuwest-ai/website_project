# Hero Headline Options — Alliterative & Punchy

## Current Version
**"Prove your resilience. Prove your AI is safe."**

---

## Option 1: Triple T (Short & Sharp)
**"Test. Track. Trust."**
- Ultra-concise, three-beat rhythm
- Action → Process → Outcome
- Memorable and repeatable

---

## Option 2: Real Results (Authenticity Focus)
**"Real Resilience. Real Results."**
- Emphasizes authenticity over theater
- Contrasts with vague industry claims
- Strong R alliteration

---

## Option 3: Battle-Ready (Three-Beat Power)
**"Battle-Tested. Board-Ready. Built Right."**
- Three B's create powerful rhythm
- Technical credibility → Business value → Quality
- Appeals to both security teams and leadership

---

## Option 4: Validate & Verify (V-Power)
**"Validate Viability. Verify Vigilance."**
- Strong V alliteration
- More formal/enterprise tone
- Dual-track messaging (security + ethical)

---

## Option 5: Test Truth (Honesty Angle)
**"Test Truth. Trust Results."**
- T alliteration
- Emphasizes evidence-based approach
- "Truth" is provocative in security space

---

## Option 6: Prove & Perfect (Action-Oriented)
**"Prove Protection. Perfect Performance."**
- P alliteration
- Active verbs
- Continuous improvement message

---

## Option 7: Security Seen (Visibility)
**"Security Seen. Safety Shown."**
- S alliteration
- Makes abstract concepts tangible
- Emphasizes transparency

---

## Option 8: Measure Matters (Direct)
**"Measure Matters. Mean It."**
- M alliteration
- Conversational but confident
- Challenges hand-waving

---

## Option 9: Evidence Everything (Comprehensive)
**"Evidence Everywhere. Every Time."**
- Triple E alliteration
- Emphasizes consistency and completeness
- On-brand with "evidence over assurances"

---

## Option 10: Resilience Reality (No Theater)
**"Resilience Reality. Results Reported."**
- R alliteration
- Anti-theater message
- Transparency emphasis

---

## Recommendation: Top 3

### 🥇 **"Test. Track. Trust."**
- Most punchy and memorable
- Works perfectly with tagline: "Evidence beats assurances"
- Three syllables = easy recall

### 🥈 **"Battle-Tested. Board-Ready. Built Right."**
- Appeals to dual audience (technical + executive)
- Strong rhythm and confidence
- Differentiates from competitors

### 🥉 **"Real Resilience. Real Results."**
- Clear anti-BS positioning
- Natural fit with bsdetector product name
- Simple but powerful
