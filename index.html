About Nuwest

Nuwest AI LLC is a California-incorporated company exploring the intersections of data, security, and human behavior. We believe the most important risks are often the ones hiding in plain sight—the dark corners where technology, psychology, and vulnerability converge.

Our work spans research into psycho-informational risk and practical tools for cyber crisis readiness. We're practitioners first: security professionals who've lived through incidents, researchers who ask uncomfortable questions, and builders who believe transparency matters.

"Nuwest" pays homage to an original vision: sustainable housing development using CNC machines, WikiHouse construction methods, and hempcrete materials. A project focused on carbon capture and accessible building. That dream evolved, but the spirit remains—building things that matter, differently.
