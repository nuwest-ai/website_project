# nuwest.ai — Mission, Vision, Values

## Mission
Turn assumptions into evidence. We help security leaders prove that their systems are resilient and that their AI does not cause harm.

## Vision
Every enterprise can verify, on demand, two things: 1) security viability against current threats, 2) ethical viability of AI agents against misuse, bias, and jailbreaks.

## What we do
We design and run adversarial, repeatable tests that expose hidden fragility across security controls, data protection, and AI agent behaviors. We deliver evidence, not dashboards.

## Differentiators
- Dual-track validation: **Security Viability** and **Ethical Viability** in one cadence.
- Realistic failure injection across identity, data, and recovery paths, not lab demos.
- AI safety tests that go beyond “safe mode” prompts to measure jailbreak resistance, harmful-action refusal, demographic-context responses, and model consistency.
- Evidence artifacts that stand up to board, audit, and regulator scrutiny.

## Values
- **Truth over theater**: No compliance cosplay. We publish what is real.
- **Minimum harm**: We design tests that discover risk while protecting people and data.
- **Adversarial empathy**: Assume an intelligent opponent and design accordingly.
- **Repeatability**: Tests are codified, versioned, and re-run on change.
- **Clarity**: Plain language. Clear evidence. Actionable fixes.

## Buyer Thesis
CISOs and Security Strategy leaders need proof, not promises. We replace vague maturity claims with tested outcomes that reduce blast radius and reputational risk.
